    Training guide

    Easy / recovery: about 5:40–6:15/km, conversational effort.

    Long runs: about 5:40–6:10/km, mostly easy.

    Steady: about 5:05–5:25/km.

    Tempo: start around 4:35–4:50/km, controlled rather than all-out.

    Strength: 30–45 minutes of squats or split squats, Romanian deadlifts, calf raises, glute work and core. Avoid exhausting your legs before the long run.

    Paces are guides, not rules. Keep most running easy. Skip the optional run first if tired, and repeat a week rather than forcing mileage if recovery is poor.
